### Overview / About (limit 2,000 characters; this text is 1,535)

```
Phenotech is a sourced research atlas of rare diseases.

About 95% of rare diseases have no approved treatment. Most are caused by a single gene, and thousands of drugs already tested in people are not being studied for them. When two diseases break the same biology, research on one can inform the other.

Phenotech joins more than 30 public sources into one map: 11,456 rare diseases, each linked to its genes, symptoms, mechanisms, variants, clinical studies, papers, patient groups and registries. Every link shows its source, and every quote is checked word for word against it. Content extracted by AI is labelled as AI.

On the same data, a ranking engine lists existing drugs against 8,723 rare diseases and gives the reason for each place. These are research hypotheses for scientists and drug developers to examine, never a claim that a drug works.

We declare every test before we run it, and we publish the ones that fail.

Who it is for:
• Families and patient groups: every disease page is free, in plain words, with patient groups and recruiting studies.
• Researchers: mechanisms, evidence, and who else works on a disease.
• Patient foundations and drug developers: evidence dossiers and drug-rescue leads for gene-defined rare diseases.

Phenotech gives general information for research and education. It is not medical advice and is not for diagnosing or treating anyone. Questions about treatment belong with a doctor or genetic counsellor.

Built in Switzerland. Started at Hack-Nation Challenge 05 (October 2026).
```
